# SCENE — 60 s launch ad · REVIEW

Deliverable: `deliverables/SCENE-launch-60s.mp4` — 1920×1080, 30 fps, 60.000 s (1800 frames), H.264 CRF 19–20 + AAC 48 kHz, 14.6 MB.

## What was checked (measured)
- `hyperframes lint`: 0 errors (1 warning kept: `rewards.html` is long because it carries three inline app screens).
- `hyperframes check`: ok — lint, runtime, layout, motion, contrast, craft, audio all pass with 0 errors.
  - Warnings kept on purpose: `line_unread` on the hook labels (brief asks for cuts of 0.40 → 0.25 s), the white-silence line and the relief tagline (their windows are fixed by the brief's timeline), and a few in-phone lines; `text_below_floor` on phone UI text (real app screens at phone scale); 6 `contrast_aa_failure` on intentionally greyed items ("Full" time slots, locked perk, muted captions).
  - The lifted UI parts deliberately overlap their dimmed originals; they carry `data-layout-allow-overlap`.
- File: `ffprobe` duration 60.000 s, 1800 frames, 1920×1080 @ 30/1, 14.6 MB (< 30 MB).
- Audio (`ffmpeg ebur128` and `hyperframes audio`): −14.2 LUFS integrated, true peak −1.3 dBTP, no clipping; SFX bus set 8 dB under the music by integrated loudness; onsets line up with the seven hook pops; steepest rise and peak at 12.5 s (the drop); measured silence 11.36–12.01 s and the fade tail from 59.68 s.
- Determinism: 3D objects rendered twice per pose came out byte-identical; every animation, 3D transform and canvas draw is a function of timeline time (no `Math.random`, `Date.now`, timers or rAF; three.js's own tokens are swapped for a seeded PRNG at bundle time).
- QA stills from the rendered MP4 at every section boundary ±0.1 s and the chat-readability moments (`review/qa/`). Found and fixed: stray gold burst rings visible 37–44 s, a ghost "₹72" beside the lifted Total at 34 s, the vignette greying the white silence, and a glow flash behind the end mark. Only those ranges were re-rendered (11.3–13.0, 33.5–44.6, 54.9–55.9 s) and spliced frame-accurately (patch edge frames match the original at 43–50 dB PSNR); the seams were inspected.

## What nobody checked
- **The sound was measured, not heard.** I can't listen; the mix, the melody and the SFX timing are verified only by loudness, onsets and a spectrogram.
- Playback on real phones/TVs, and editing the project in the HyperFrames desktop app.
- WebGL was rendered with software GL in a container; a GPU render may differ by tiny amounts in anti-aliasing.

## Changes from the brief (all deliberate or requested)
- **Look (by your direction):** near-black ground (#06080D) instead of the light glassmorphism world; no glow (blobs, auras, halos, flash removed). Secondary colour **Sun Yellow #FFD23F** as the solid complement to blue: hero words, ✓ SORTED pills, tag dots and wires, the 3D second rim, end particles. Hero words are solid yellow rather than the blue→cyan gradient.
- The film ends on black instead of white; the "THERE'S AN EASIER WAY." beat stays a white silence.
- Wordmark renders white → light blue on the dark ground (the SVG files in `assets/brand/` keep the specified navy → blue).
- Dark phones on a dark ground get a hairline edge to separate them.
- Gold is no longer the only warm colour (the yellow secondary is close to it); gold still marks the level-up badge and Coins.
- Claw-machine glass transmission 0.95 (0.9 washed out the plushes); emissive cyan parts bypass tone mapping to stay exact #36D6F2.
- Sample story additions: Level 10 unlocks at 3,330 XP ("100 XP to Level 10" on Home); four invented venues (Ten Pin Theory, Last Over Box Cricket, Cipher Vault Escapes, Lapline Karting — checked against real brands); Off-peak ₹199; booking ID HV8-22N-603; a "Check this" message before the shared card.
- The Venue screen (VR headset hero) plays for 1.2 s before Packages in the "Per person?" beat.
- One mastered mix is on the timeline; the stems are regenerated by `tools/make-audio.mjs`.

## Weakest shot / with more time
- The answers phones sit at ~1.0× scale; in-phone text is small at 1080p even though the lifted parts and tags carry the read. A tighter push-in per beat would help.
- The coins in the rewards beat read pale against black.
- The reward rays at 46 s are the one remaining soft burst; they could become crisp solid rays.
