# Organic Maps — storylines (unofficial concept film)

The one thing: **maps that keep working when the signal doesn't: offline, detailed, private and free.**
Sources: the app's own Play/F-Droid listing in github.com/organicmaps/organicmaps (copy, features, screenshots, logo).

## A. No signal, no problem (built)

Device: a question answered by the product. Form: one continuous 3D world; the camera flies down a valley the app's hiking route is drawn through, then meets the phone hovering over it.
- First 2 s: "No signal." on a pale ground; "No data." swaps in.
- Beats: hook → flight down the valley (route draws ahead, pins pop) → phone lands, "Organic | Maps" → 4 features on real screens → "No ads. No tracking. 100% free." over the finished route → mark, name, organicmaps.app, credits.
- Look: the app's outdoor-map palette made 3D (meadow and forest greens, brown contour lines, navy dashed route, red pins), pale sky, Organic Maps green #006C35 as the accent, Inter for the iOS system face.
- Sound: "Familiar Roads" (Tanner Helland, CC BY 4.0) from 145.5 s, beats on the key moments; four Pixabay effects.
