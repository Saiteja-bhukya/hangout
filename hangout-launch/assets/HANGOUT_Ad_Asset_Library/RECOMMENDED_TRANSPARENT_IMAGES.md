## Notes

- **Many of these images are AI-generated.** Adobe labels them "Generated with AI". That is fine for an ad, but for the thesis say they are licensed stock images and that some are AI-generated.
- **Keep one look.** The Adobe picks are photoreal; the Vecteezy kart is a stylised 3D illustration. For the ad, choose one style for all of them. Re-search the same site if one item doesn't match.
- **"Transparent" in a title is not proof.** One Adobe bowling-pin listing (1548258633) said "transparent background" in its title but downloads only as a JPEG. Check that the download is a PNG with alpha.
- **Avoid PNG aggregator sites** such as hiclipart, nicepng, pngitem and seekpng. Their licensing is unclear.
- **Swapping them into the film:** this workspace cannot download from these sites. Attach the PNGs you choose and they can replace the stylised renders in the film's discovery "portal" and in the category tiles.
