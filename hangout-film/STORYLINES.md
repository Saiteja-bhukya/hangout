# Hangout — storylines

The one thing: **Hangout turns a weekend with no plan into one you can find, book and walk into, and every visit earns you something.**
Sources: the Concept Note (survey numbers, the problem) and Progress Review 2 (the app's real screens, features, copy and the January alpha test). Every UI shown is a real screen from the test build.

## A. Saturday, solved (built)

Device: life before and after, then the feature as the payoff. Form: one continuous 3D stage; the real iPhone never leaves it.
Black type opens on the problem ("It's Saturday." / "40 messages later, still no plan." / 68% missed a good venue). The app's own world arrives as glossy teal VR, bowling and arcade objects drifting through the dark; they part, a phone turns from its back to reveal Hangout. The phone then walks through Find → Know the crowd → Book → Walk in → Level up, and Hangout Coins burst out of the screen and orbit it.

- First 2 s: "It's Saturday." in white on black, the word "Saturday." blurring in.
- Beats: hook → stat → objects dive → phone reveal "Meet Hangout." → 5 verbs on real screens → coins payoff "Make every outing count." → logo, tagline, alpha date.
- Look: near-black studio, one teal pool of light, glossy teal and gold objects, Space Grotesk headlines, Inter body (the deck's own faces).
- Sound: no music bed available here; five Pixabay effects on visible actions (riser into impact on the reveal, whooshes on the two big phone moves, pops on the coins).

## B. The empty weekday (not built)

Venue-side film for owners: Palace's floor at 30–50 visitors on a weekday against 100–200 on a weekend, the venue app filling empty slots with off-peak deals, the free tool, the commission only on new customers. Needs the venue app's designed screens (only wireframes exist today).

## C. One outing, five friends (not built)

A group chat that fails to decide, then "Plan an outing" building one plan for five people with group prices and split bills. Needs the split-payment screen, which is still in progress.
