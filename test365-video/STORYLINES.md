# Test365 — storylines

The one thing: **Test365 is one place to prepare for JEE, NEET, GATE and IIT JAM: notes, past papers, mock tests, analytics, a mistake notebook and leaderboards.**
Every line on screen is copy from Test365's own walkthrough video; every product screen is a still from that recording.

## A. One window that rebuilds (built)

Device: a question with the input. Form: object-to-object chain.
The exam the viewer is preparing for rolls through a slot, the need lands as a checklist, the Test365 mark slams in and flies into the site's own nav bar, and the homepage window then becomes a fixed stage that swaps through six real features.

- First 2 s: "Are you preparing for" sits on the pastel ground; the last words roll JEE Main → NEET → GATE CS.
- Beats: hook (exam slot) → need (checklist) → reveal (logo slam) → carry (logo into nav) → homepage → 6 features in one window → end card with a clicked URL button.
- Look: Test365's own pastel blob ground, Inter 900 ink, brand blue #0040FF on one word per shot.
- Sound: no music bed (see REVIEW.md); five Pixabay effects on visible actions only.
